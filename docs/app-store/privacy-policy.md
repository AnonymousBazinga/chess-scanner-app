# Chess Scanner Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

Chess Scanner does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Photos.** Photos you take or choose are processed on your device to read the
  chess position. They are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Camera and photo library.** Access is used only to get the board photo you
  ask the app to scan.
- **History.** Positions you analyze are saved on your device only. Deleting the
  app deletes them.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app makes no network
  requests of its own. The optional "Open in Lichess" action opens lichess.org in
  your browser, where Lichess's own privacy policy applies.
