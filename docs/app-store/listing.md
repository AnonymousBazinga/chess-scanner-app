## Description (4000)

Take a photo of a real chessboard and analyze the position with Stockfish 17.

Chess Scanner reads the pieces from your photo, lets you fix anything it got
wrong, and shows the engine's evaluation and top three lines, the way you're
used to on analysis boards.

SCAN
• Photograph any physical board, from any angle, or pick a photo from your library
• Board recognition runs entirely on your device

REVIEW
• Drag pieces to correct the scan, or tap a piece and tap squares to place it
• Compare with your photo at a glance
• Illegal positions are flagged before you analyze

ANALYZE
• Stockfish 17, running on your phone, no internet needed
• Evaluation bar, top three engine lines and a best-move arrow
• Play moves on the board and the analysis follows
• Positions you analyze are saved to History

PRIVATE
Photos never leave your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
